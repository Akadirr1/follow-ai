### Prototype-parity limitation, not classified as a defect

Plain ECMAScript folding is not Turkish-locale-aware. Independent reproduction:

```text
> node -e "const q='İLAÇ'.toLowerCase(); const hay='ilaç'.toLowerCase(); console.log(JSON.stringify({query:q,corpus:hay,matches:hay.includes(q)}));"
{"query":"i̇laç","corpus":"ilaç","matches":false}
```

Thus `İLAÇ` does not match `ilaç`. This is a real search limitation, but the change intentionally restores the prototype's exact `toLowerCase()` behavior and rev-001 explicitly deferred broader multilingual folding to a separately specified policy. I therefore agree it is prototype-parity rather than an impl-002 defect.

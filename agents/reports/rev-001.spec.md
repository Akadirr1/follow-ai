## Input
The evidence pack is your input. It names the exact base (empty tree), the 36 files
under review, the prototype line ranges, the structural neighbourhood from the code

## Mandates
1. **Diff correctness.** Trace each behaviour in the prototype's `renderVals()`
   (design file lines 342–403) to its implementation in `src/store/reducer.ts`,
   `src/store/selectors.ts`, and the screen that dispatches it. Check failure paths:
   unknown ids, empty lists, rapid repeated presses, toast replacement, sheet
   cancel-vs-save, source toggles combined with chip filters, font-load failure.
   Confirm every swallowed state warns (brief requirement) and that no warn is
   attached to the wrong transition.
2. **Structural impact.** For each consumer listed in pack §3, decide whether the
   store's contract (`State`, `Action`, selector signatures, `useStore`/`useDispatch`)
   is used consistently, and whether a change in one screen's assumptions would
   break another's. Pay attention to `StoreProvider` being imported by `Toast` and
   `DigestTimeSheet` as well as every screen.
3. **Parity and constraints.** Judge the nine implementer decisions in pack §6 on
   their merits against the prototype; verify the invariants (pack §4) and the
   brief's constraints (mock data verbatim, initial state, no network/persistence,
   exact versions, < 500 lines, forbidden paths). Spot-check at least ten UI strings
   and all five articles' fields against the prototype yourself rather than trusting
   the 82/82 + 72/72 claim.

**blocking defects**, **non-blocking debt**, and **questions**. If there are no
blocking findings, state precisely what you inspected and what remains unverified.
Do not merge, do not approve deployment, do not update task status.
